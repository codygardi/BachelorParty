    Saturday route

    1 pm departure. Meet at Bubba’s by 12:45. Allow 75–90 minutes for the drive and parking. Leave by 2 pm at the latest.

    Drinks and escape room. The Snug opens at 4 pm. Leave by 4:50 for Séance’s 5:15 check-in or by 5:10 for Tortuga’s 5:30 check-in. Take both cars to Tortuga. After Séance, allow time for the drivers to reach the cars.

    Dinner. Aim for 7 pm after Séance if the cars are parked at the room, or 7:20 pm after Tortuga. Walk from Pete’s or Thai Terrace. Wildwood and Tapa the World are a short drive. Flip each dinner card for directions, and leave dinner by 8:25 pm.

    Show. Arrive together by 8:45 pm at 2100 Arden Way. Bring photo ID. Two beverages per guest, including alcohol-free options.

    Weekend cap. $250 per person before restaurant tips. Punch Line general admission lists $32.05 before tax, plus two drinks.
